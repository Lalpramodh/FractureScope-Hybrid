Fracture Types Explained

1. Elbow Fracture

Often caused by falls or direct trauma. Symptoms include pain, swelling, reduced mobility, and tenderness around the elbow joint.

2. Forearm Fracture

Affects the radius and/or ulna bones. Common in sports injuries or accidents. Symptoms include intense pain, bruising, and visible deformity.

3. Shoulder Fracture

Usually involves the clavicle or humerus near the shoulder. Signs include shoulder pain, swelling, and inability to lift the arm.

4. Hand Fracture

Can affect the fingers, metacarpals, or wrist bones. Typical symptoms include pain, stiffness, and difficulty gripping objects.

What Should You Do Next?

  • Consult a Doctor: Always seek immediate medical attention for a suspected fracture.
  • Diagnostic Confirmation: Further imaging such as X-ray, CT scan, or MRI may be recommended by your doctor.
  • Orthopedic Evaluation: An orthopedic specialist can assess the severity and determine the need for casting, splinting, or surgery.

Treatment & Medications

  • Pain Relievers: NSAIDs (e.g., ibuprofen) or acetaminophen to manage pain and inflammation.
  • Immobilization: Using a cast, splint, or brace to allow proper bone healing.
  • Physical Therapy: Helps restore mobility and strength after immobilization.
  • Surgery: In severe cases, internal fixation using plates, screws, or rods may be necessary.

Note: This AI-based prediction is intended to support clinical decisions, not replace professional medical advice. Please consult a licensed physician for accurate diagnosis and treatment.

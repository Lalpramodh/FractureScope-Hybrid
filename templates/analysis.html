Hybrid AI workflow

X-ray↓YOLOv8 Detection↓Fracture Localization↓Bounding Box Crop↓Swin Transformer Classification↓Hybrid Result

Each detected region is cropped with a small boundary margin and classified independently. The result keeps the YOLOv8 bounding box and detection confidence beside the Swin Transformer class and classification confidence.

What the screening means

The model output is a useful prompt for review, but it should always be evaluated alongside symptoms, examination, and appropriate imaging.

Common areas

  • Elbow: pain, swelling, tenderness, and reduced mobility after a fall or impact.
  • Forearm: radius or ulna injuries can present with bruising, pain, and visible deformity.
  • Shoulder: clavicle, scapula, or upper humerus injuries may limit arm movement.
  • Hand: finger, metacarpal, or wrist injuries can affect grip and alignment.

What to do next

  • Seek professional review: suspected fractures need timely medical attention.
  • Confirm the finding: a clinician may recommend additional X-ray views, CT, or MRI.
  • Follow the care plan: treatment can include immobilization, medication, therapy, or surgery.

The project

Designed for the moment between image and action.

Reference anatomy

Common fracture locations.

These areas are included as practical context for interpreting a screening result.

Wrist

Distal radius injuries are common after a fall onto an outstretched hand.

Elbow

Falls and direct impacts can affect the radial head, olecranon, or distal humerus.

Humerus

Upper-arm fractures can involve the proximal, shaft, or distal sections.

Shoulder

Trauma may affect the clavicle, scapula, or upper part of the humerus.

Hand

Finger and metacarpal fractures can change grip, alignment, and movement.
